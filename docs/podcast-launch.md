# BriefBerlin podcast launch

**BriefBerlin – Einfache Nachrichten aus Berlin** publishes one episode per German B1 article with podcast-ready audio. The public subscription URL after deployment is `https://briefberlin.de/podcast/b1.xml`. Each episode uses the article recording and links back to the B1 text. The written synthetic-voice disclosure belongs in the RSS show and episode descriptions only.

## First deployment

1. After GitHub Pages deploys the feed, open its public URL. Verify that the initial catalog includes the Hugohaus, Berliner Wahlkarte, and Wohnungsmarkt stories. Open the cover image and article links from the feed.
2. Follow the feed by URL in a podcast app. Play an episode and seek into the recording. Verify the displayed title, cover, episode notes, and matching article.
3. After the next B1 article with uploaded MP3 audio is published, refresh the subscription. Verify that exactly one new episode arrives and that rebuilding the site preserves its episode ID.

## Directory listing

Use the same public feed URL for each submission. Use a publisher-controlled email address accessible to the person verifying ownership.

- Submit the feed in [Apple Podcasts Connect](https://podcastsconnect.apple.com/). Review the validation result and record the listing URL or review feedback. Apple requires prominent AI disclosure in content and metadata. Acceptance of written feed-only disclosure for this audio show is uncertain. Bring any request for a narration change back to the publisher. [Apple content guidelines](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/891-content-and-subscription-guidelines)
- In [Spotify for Creators](https://creators.spotify.com/), add the existing externally hosted show by RSS URL and verify ownership. Record the public show URL once listed.
- If YouTube distribution is desired, connect the feed in YouTube Studio and verify which episodes will be imported. [YouTube RSS ingestion](https://support.google.com/youtubemusic/answer/13525207)

## First-month review

Record listener counts alongside the available completion and returning-listener figures per platform. Review which B1 stories listeners finish and whether they come back for later episodes.
