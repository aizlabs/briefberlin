name: briefberlin-review
description: Explain a BriefBerlin Tier 2 editorial review card and its authenticated Telegram controls.
---

The host dispatch service sends the immutable case to the configured editor and handles its buttons directly. Explain the Berlin impact, reason, original link, score and evidence version displayed on the card. Only the authorized Telegram sender can act on controls in that exact message.

Approve, Decline and Hold act on the story and revision named on the card. Rate 0–10 records feedback. Rate + publish (4–10) explicitly combines a rating and approval. A plain chat message cannot sign an action. An observation card exercises decisions privately in the observation ledger. Live approval wakes the fenced publisher and still requires successful generation, quality checks, a valid claim and verified deployment.

If the card expired or was superseded, the editor can request a fresh manual preview using the briefberlin skill. For a missing or ambiguous card, direct the operator to the managed outbox and explicit resend procedure in the discovery handover. Never send a replacement card, sign an event or infer a successful publication from the button acknowledgment.
