A new chapter is beginning on Kurfürstendamm in Berlin-Charlottenburg. The Italian restaurant Circolo Popolare is opening in the former Café Kranzler on Friday evening. It has three floors and room for around 250 guests. Much else is changing at the so-called Kranzler Eck. The burger chain Goldies plans to open soon in a neighboring space that used to belong to the café. A roof garden and a venue for events in the courtyard are also being created. Large birdcages once stood there. A Rossmann drugstore is also opening on November 28. But some people are already criticizing its large red illuminated letters. They say the historic Kranzler sign on the round tower, the rotunda, is now barely visible.

For some Berliners, saying goodbye to the historic coffeehouse is surely difficult. Even so, its decline began about 25 years ago. At that time, the Neue Kranzler Eck was built to designs by architect Helmut Jahn. After that, the Viennese-style café remained only in the rotunda. Most recently, the rotunda, too, stood empty for two years. The owner, the French insurance company Axa, probably did not want to reopen the café. After all, the café-bar The Dawn opened on Ku'damm at the beginning of the year, and there are other cafés in the arcade.

The Big Mamma Group is behind the new restaurant. Two Frenchmen founded it in Paris. Today, it has about 36 restaurants in ten countries. Its German subsidiary already runs two restaurants in Berlin-Mitte. Most of the employees come from Italy. The rooms are richly decorated, for example with over 1000 old liqueur and wine bottles. There are also strings of lights, artificial plants, and colorful wooden and wicker chairs. Pasta and pizza start at 13 euros.

Some things from the past remain, however: the red awnings, the curved staircase, and the Sacher cake for 11.50 euros. In summer, guests can once again sit outside and on the balcony. The rotunda can be rented for celebrations. The Austrian pastry chef Johann Georg Kranzler founded the café on Unter den Linden in 1825. The café in the west of the city was added in 1932.
