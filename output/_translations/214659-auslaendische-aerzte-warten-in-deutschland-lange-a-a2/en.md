Foreign doctors wait a long time to work in Germany

Germany needs more doctors from abroad. But doctors from outside the EU often wait years. First, government offices check their degrees.

Many doctors in Germany are already old. At the end of 2025, one in four doctors was 60 or older. Patients already wait a long time for appointments. Many doctors' offices have too much work.

Ghassan Al-Shalak is a dentist from Syria. He came to Berlin in 2015. Then he waited five years for a full license. Today he has his own dental office in Berlin-Kreuzberg.

There are two ways. Experts check the certificates, or the doctors take an exam. The first way is expensive and often takes a long time. The dentist Zeynep Çınar from Istanbul handed in her papers in Bavaria in 2024. In March 2026, the office said no. She paid about 7,000 euros.

The exam is not quick either. Many doctors wait more than a year for an appointment. But the law says they should wait no more than six months. During this time, some doctors work as taxi drivers or in snack bars. A group of doctors therefore wants one office for all of Germany.

This is a big problem. Many doctors do not want to come to Germany. Almost half of them say: We want to go to a country with less paperwork. Also, right-wing parties are getting stronger. Some doctors are therefore thinking about leaving. Even so, almost 8,000 doctors from Syria without German passports are already working in Germany today.
