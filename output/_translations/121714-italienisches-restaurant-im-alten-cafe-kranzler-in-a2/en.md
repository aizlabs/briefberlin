On Friday evening, a new Italian restaurant opens in Berlin. It is called “Circolo Popolare” and is in the old Café Kranzler. The building is on Kurfürstendamm, a well-known street in Berlin-Charlottenburg.

The restaurant has three floors and room for about 250 guests. Pasta and pizza start at 13 euros. More than 1000 old bottles from Italy hang on the walls. Most of the staff also come from Italy. Two Frenchmen started the company behind the restaurant in Paris.

The company is called Big Mamma Group. It has about 36 restaurants in ten countries. It already has two other restaurants in Berlin-Mitte. But some old things are staying in Café Kranzler. The red awnings and the stairs are still there, and there is still Sacher cake.

More things are changing at Kranzler Eck. A burger restaurant will soon open there. From 28 November, there will also be a Rossmann drugstore. Some people are annoyed about the red Rossmann advertising. They say: You can no longer see the old name “Kranzler” on the building very well.

The café has a long history. The Austrian cake maker Johann Georg Kranzler started it in 1825. The café on Kurfürstendamm has been there since 1932. But about 25 years ago, it became much smaller. Most recently, it was empty for two years, and there are already many other cafés nearby.
