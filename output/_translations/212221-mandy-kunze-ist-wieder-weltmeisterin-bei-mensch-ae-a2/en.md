On Saturday, the fourth world championship in “Mensch ärgere dich nicht” took place in Berlin. It is a well-known board game played with dice. Mandy Kunze from Saxony won again.

172 people played at 43 tables in a hotel. Most of them come from Germany. Some also come from the Netherlands, Austria and Switzerland. The youngest player was seven years old. The oldest person was almost 90 years old.

The company Schmidt Spiele organized the tournament. It has made the game since 1915. The company has sold about 100 million copies of it. Today, about 400,000 people buy it every year. Many young people also like the game. They want a break from their phones and computers.

First, there were four rounds. The players got points in these rounds. Then the four best players played in the final. The company’s team says: How you play is important. But you cannot practise for the game. For the company, it is also a game for talking.

Christel Haase is 77 years old and comes from Potsdam. She says: “I don’t have to win. But the game keeps my mind fit.”

Mandy Kunze is 50 years old. She was already world champion two years ago. Her way of playing is simple. She quietly moves one piece past the others. After about 30 minutes, she had all four pieces at the finish. The others had only one or two pieces at the finish. Kunze got a trophy. She also got a voucher for a trip. It is worth 250 euros.
