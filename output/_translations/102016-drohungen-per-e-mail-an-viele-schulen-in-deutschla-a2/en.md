On Monday, hundreds of schools in Germany received a threat by email. In Berlin, 14 schools were affected. Unknown people wrote in the emails: We have put bombs in the school.

Other federal states had even more cases. In Brandenburg, more than 170 schools received an email like this. In Baden-Württemberg, it was over 450 schools, and in Saxony-Anhalt, about 260. Other federal states and Denmark also reported cases. The emails are almost all the same.

The police believe there is no real danger. Even so, they are checking every case carefully. They are also speaking to the school heads. In Brandenburg, some schools cleared their buildings. This means that all the children left the building. But now, classes are back to normal almost everywhere there.

In Berlin, the school day is going on as normal. Stephan Weh is a leader at the police union in Berlin. This group helps police officers with problems at work. He says: The people behind the threats want to scare people. Even so, the threats must always be checked carefully.

This is not the first time there have been threats like these. In November 2025, about 20 schools in Berlin had a similar problem. Two weeks ago, around 6300 schools in Italy received threats. But nothing happened there. There have also been emails like these in Scotland, Northern Ireland and Ireland. Italy's education minister says: We need to be careful. Some states want to make other countries feel unsafe with actions like these.
