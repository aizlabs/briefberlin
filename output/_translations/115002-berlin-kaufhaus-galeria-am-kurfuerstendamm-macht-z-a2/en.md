The Galeria department store on Kurfürstendamm in Berlin is closing. The company Galeria said this on Friday. Kurfürstendamm is a well-known shopping street in the west of Berlin.

First, the company told the workers at a meeting. The workers were very sad and unsure. That is why the department store was closed afterwards. It opened again on Saturday. It will stay open as usual until February. After that, everything is unclear. A young sales assistant cannot find a new job because other department stores are closing too. She wants to leave Berlin.

The department store has six floors. Everything looks normal there. There are special offers, and many people are already shopping for Christmas.

The trade union Verdi strongly criticises Galeria. A trade union helps workers. Silke Zimmer from Verdi says: The company has not put any money into the department stores for decades. The workers have gone without pay for a long time. They want to keep their jobs. Galeria has no money left and is bankrupt. Verdi says: Now a new investor must save all 83 department stores.

Many customers do not understand the decision either. The department store has been there since 1971. It used to be called Wertheim, then Karstadt. It has always sold a lot. One customer therefore believes: The bosses made mistakes. Another customer says: The nearby fashion store Peek & Cloppenburg may close too. A married couple is afraid: The city centres are becoming empty.

There are other shops in the building too. The pet shop Fressnapf will stay for another five years. The workers at Lidl know nothing about a closure. Galeria's rental agreement ends in spring 2027. After that, the owner Stumpf Development wants to build something new there. The owner is probably planning a tall building with flats and shops.
