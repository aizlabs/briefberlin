The company Freenow from Hamburg has an app for taxis. Soon, Freenow wants to offer private hire cars with drivers again in Berlin. These are cars with drivers like those at Uber. Freenow also plans to do this in other German cities.

Almost two years ago, Freenow stopped offering these rides. At the time, the boss Andreas Maier said: The prices are too low. The companies make hardly any money, and there is often illegal work. Since January 2025, people in Berlin have only been able to order taxis through the app.

Freenow used to be called Mytaxi. In 2025, the US company Lyft bought Freenow. Lyft boss David Risher announced the new plan. He also said: The name Lyft will slowly replace the name Freenow.

Manager Alexander Mönch says: Taxis remain the most important. But some customers think taxis are too expensive. Freenow does not want to lose these customers to Uber and Bolt. But in 2024, Mönch himself warned about these ride services. He said: The drivers get too little money. Taxis have fixed prices. There is no minimum price for private hire cars. Today, Mönch says: The offices can now spot false papers. Also, the cars almost always have customers.

But not everyone believes that. In Brandenburg, the public prosecutor's office is looking for information about two people who work at an office. It believes: Private hire car companies may have given them money. But this has not yet been proven. A report for the city of Berlin from the start of 2026 says: The low prices are only possible with illegal work. A study for Uber said something different in 2024.

Mönch now says: Cities should set minimum prices for private hire cars. Some people think this is a good idea. They hope: Then the competition with taxis will be fairer.
