Stadtbad Schöneberg in Berlin has been open again since September. After a few weeks, there are problems there again. Some areas are closed, and the elevator is not working.

First, the pool opened again for guests in September. The construction work in the building took about two and a half years. Before that, the pool was supposed to open at the end of August. But experts found some problems during the test.

The fun area, warm whirlpools, and a pool with salt water stay closed. During the fall holidays, very many guests came to the pool. So the pool had to let in fewer people at times. The operators say: The technology had too much work because of that.

Now experts are checking the technology for clean water. They are also setting the pumps in the pool better. This is needed because old and new technology must work together. The broken elevator probably has another reason.

A part on the elevator is damaged, and a repair is planned. The operators say: The problems should last only a short time. A fast-food restaurant in the old pool also bothers some guests. Some guests there often smell the food strongly.

So the pool is checking a better ventilation system for the restaurant. It is talking about this with companies and the tenant. At the start of 2024, the pool had to close because of bad water. After that, the city spent about 14 million euros.
