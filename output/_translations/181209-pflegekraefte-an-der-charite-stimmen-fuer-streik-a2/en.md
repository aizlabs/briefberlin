There is now a conflict at Charité in Berlin. Charité is a large hospital in the city. Nurses want better working conditions and are threatening a strike.

Working conditions are the rules and the situation at work. The nurses say: Their work should get better. The union Verdi supports many nurses in this conflict. A union helps workers in talks with the employer.

First, Verdi asked its members at Charité. The question was: Are you ready for a strike? 98 percent of the members who took part said yes. That is almost all the people in this group.

A strike means: People do not work for a time. In this way they put pressure on the management of Charité. This is about a strike with no fixed end. It can last longer if there is no solution.

Now Verdi and the management are still talking with each other. The management decides a lot about the work in the hospital. If both sides do not find an agreement, Verdi can call for a strike. The yes from the members gives Verdi a lot of strength for this. That is why the pressure on the management of Charité is now growing.

Verdi does not say how many members it has there. But on many wards, many nurses work together with Verdi. A ward is an area in a hospital. That is why the result is important for the talks.
