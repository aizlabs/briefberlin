There is a suspicion in the Teltow-Fläming district in Brandenburg. Two employees of the district office may have taken money for licences. The prosecutor's office in Neuruppin has been looking for information since August.

The prosecutor's office is an authority that deals with crimes. In Neuruppin, it deals especially with bribery. The two employees worked in a department for hire cars. Hire cars are cheap cars with a driver. People order them using apps like Uber and Bolt. The prosecutor's office believes the employees gave out licences in exchange for money. Seven other people may also have paid money. Most of them are from Berlin. But no one has been found guilty yet.

There are very many hire cars in the district. From the end of 2025 to the middle of 2026, the number almost doubled. Now there are more than 2,100 cars. The state government said this. SPD politician Martina Maxi Schmidt asked about it. Only about 177,000 people live in the district. Berlin has almost four million residents. But in March, Berlin had only 1,656 hire cars.

The reason is a scandal in Berlin in 2024. At that time, Berlin stopped 1,661 hire cars. The companies had not registered the cars properly. So they did not pay taxes. After that, many companies gave up. But many new companies appeared in Brandenburg. Their cars still drive in Berlin. Experts and taxi drivers warned early on that the problems were just moving to another place.

The clues came from the district office itself. A spokesperson said that the office had checked things itself and employees had given tips. The two employees are not allowed to work at the moment. Investigators searched homes and offices in Berlin and Brandenburg. Now they are checking documents and data. The district office is helping them. Since the scandal, Berlin and Brandenburg have also been working closely together, including against illegal work.
