A large building for students is being built in Berlin-Spandau. It is on the Havel river, near the old town. For a long time, the construction site was quiet, but now work is continuing. There will be 300 small apartments with furniture there.

Work began in 2022. The building was originally planned for 2024. But there have been many problems. No one worked on the building for more than a year. After that, the company Berlinovo left the project, and Berlinovo belongs to the state of Berlin.

Since the summer, the company Home & Co has been in charge of the project. The company rents out apartments for students in Germany and Spain. The building is now called “Berlin Riverside”. There is a kitchen for everyone, a gym and a room for computer games. But living there is not cheap: an 18-square-metre apartment costs 829 euros a month.

The company says: The first students can move in in April 2027. But that is not certain. Part of the building is still missing, and the garden is not finished either. The location is good. It takes five minutes to walk to Bahnhof Spandau. From there, trains go to the universities in Berlin and Potsdam.

The place also has a history. About 150 years ago, there was a church cemetery here. Before building began, the dead were taken to the church and buried there again. There were also parts of a well at the construction site. The plan was: The well would be built again in the courtyard. But now the well is probably no longer there.
