On Friday, thousands of young people took to the streets in Berlin. There were also protests in many other German cities. The young people are against compulsory military service. Compulsory military service means that young people have to join the army.

The group “Gen Z regelt” organized the protest. It also wants more money for schools. People with drug problems or mental health problems should get more help. Life should not be so expensive. And the group wants more action to protect the climate.

In Berlin, people walked from Alexanderplatz to Mariannenplatz. They did not set off until 1:30 p.m. That was two hours later than planned. The police counted about 4,000 people. The organizers say: There were 10,000 to 12,000 people.

The police say: The protest was peaceful. Many of the people there were students. But members of left-wing parties and groups for Palestine were there too. Felix Banaszak is the leader of Die Grünen. He supported the young people. He says: Life is getting more and more expensive. That is hard for young people.

There was a second demonstration at Brandenburger Tor. Student representatives from all over Germany protested there. They want better school buildings and more help for teachers. This group does not work with “Gen Z regelt”.

Akram Selo helps organize the protests. He says: We want clear promises from politicians. Just talking is not enough. Selo also does not want any violence. There were already large protests on October 31. But in the evening there was violence in some cities, for example in Frankfurt. At that time, more than 180,000 people signed up. This time, there were only about 21,000 in 73 cities.
