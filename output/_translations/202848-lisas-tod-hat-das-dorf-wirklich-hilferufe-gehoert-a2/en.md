Lisa’s death: Did the village really hear cries for help?

A man killed 13-year-old Lisa from Berlin. The girl had been missing for days. On Sunday, police officers found Lisa dead by chance on a farm. The farm is in Altglietzen. This is a small village in Brandenburg, near Poland.

The police believe a 22-year-old German man is the killer. A court has punished him before. The police have also been checking for years: Does he have illegal pictures of children? The police do not yet know everything about the crime.

The newspaper “Bild” was the first to arrive in the village. It wrote that neighbors heard cries for help. But they did not call the police. Many other media outlets wrote this too. But they did not speak to the people themselves.

Because of this, many people on the internet criticized the village. On Monday, many reporters came to Altglietzen. But no resident said: I heard screams. Not even the next-door neighbors. Everyone was shocked and sad.

The public prosecutor’s office in Berlin also has no proof of the cries for help. It works with the police on crimes. On Tuesday, the police questioned all the neighbors. It is likely that no one told them about screams.

But a married couple spoke about screams as early as Sunday evening. Their house is by a path through the woods. Maybe the killer dragged Lisa along this path to the farm. Is their statement true? That is not certain.

Sometimes, after a crime, witnesses believe: I heard something. They are not lying, but they are wrong. But maybe the couple really did hear Lisa. Then it is unclear: Why did they not get help? The couple did not answer questions.

One thing is clear: The whole village is not to blame. The people in Altglietzen met in their church. They put candles and flowers there. Together, they remembered Lisa.
