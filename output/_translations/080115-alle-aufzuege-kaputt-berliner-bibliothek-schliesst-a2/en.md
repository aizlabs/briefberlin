The Amerika-Gedenkbibliothek in Berlin-Kreuzberg has a big problem. All the elevators in the building are broken. So the popular lounge is now closed to visitors.

The lounge is on the ground floor next to the entrance. People usually drink coffee and read newspapers there. The art library is also there. At the art library, people can borrow pictures and sculptures for free. At the moment, people cannot use the art library or the magazines. The library closed the lounge on Vorlesetag, of all days. On this day, many families with children visit libraries.

Without elevators, the staff cannot take books to the other floors. So the library is now collecting all the returned and ordered books in the lounge. This means orders take longer. The last backup elevator is also broken. There is no new elevator yet. People in wheelchairs can no longer get to the offices either. The library warns: The whole building may soon have to close for a while.

The library says: The building has needed repairs for many years. But nobody has done them. When it rains, the staff put out buckets. The building is also too small. It has room for about 500 people. But around 3000 visitors come every day.

Director Jonas Fansa says: Berlin needs a good central library soon. Otherwise, the city's largest public library may have to close forever. He therefore wants a new place at Alexanderplatz. There is a large department store there. The state of Berlin and the owner of the building have already signed a plan. The library has two buildings. Both are to move into the department store. The department store will stay in part of the building.
