Several hundred people said goodbye to Lisa T. in Berlin-Marzahn. The girl was 13 years old. Someone killed her.

The memorial service was in a funeral home. This company organizes funerals. The service was private. The company says: We have held services for families from Vietnam for many years. Lisa's family also comes from Vietnam.

Many people brought flowers. One woman had a cushion with a photo of Lisa. People on the street also stopped for a short time. The police say: Around 500 people came in the morning. They went into the building one after another.

The police were there with 60 officers. A spokesperson says: Everything was calm, and nobody caused trouble. A TV station reports: Lisa's body will be cremated after the service. After that, Lisa will have a grave in Germany.

Lisa disappeared in Marzahn on 3 November. Hundreds of police officers searched for her. Dogs, a helicopter and an underwater drone also helped. Then someone reported: A stranger is in an empty house. So the police went to the house.

The house is in Altglietzen. The village is part of the town of Bad Freienwalde in Brandenburg. The police found Lisa dead there on 8 November. A week ago, dozens of people mourned Lisa in the village church in Altglietzen.

The police believe: A 22-year-old German man killed Lisa. The police arrested him in the house. He shot himself in the head. He died later. The public prosecutor's office says this. It brings criminals to court.

It is still not clear: How did Lisa die? Doctors examined her body. But they did not find an answer. So the public prosecutor's office wants more tests. The results are not ready yet. The police are also checking more than 200 tips from people.
