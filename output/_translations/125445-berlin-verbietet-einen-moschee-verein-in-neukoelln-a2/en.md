The Berlin government banned a mosque association on Tuesday. The association is called El-Irschad. Its rooms are mostly in the Berlin district of Neukölln. Three smaller groups belonging to the association are now banned too.

The police came at six in the morning. About 40 police officers searched the association’s rooms. These include a well-known center on Flughafenstraße. The police also searched the homes of important members in other districts.

Iris Spranger from the SPD party is the politician in charge of safety in Berlin. She says: The association helps Hisbollah with money and organization. It is against people from different nations living together in peace. It also spreads ideas against the constitution. The constitution is the most important set of rules in Germany. The association mainly used the mosque and its work with young people for this.

The members are no longer allowed to run the association. The state has taken the association’s money. The association’s symbols are banned in public. The authorities are also shutting down its website.

Hisbollah is an Islamic group from Lebanon. It works closely with Iran. It is part of the government in Lebanon. But it also has its own fighters. Lately, Israel has made it weaker with attacks. Hisbollah has been banned as a terrorist group in Germany since March 2020. It probably has more than 1000 supporters here.

The police did not search another center in Neukölln. It is on Harzer Straße and has a mosque and a day care center. Many politicians have criticized this center for a long time.
