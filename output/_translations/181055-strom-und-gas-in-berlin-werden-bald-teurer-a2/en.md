Many people in Berlin will soon have to pay more for energy. The company Gasag is making gas more expensive from January. From 1 February 2027, the company Vattenfall will also raise the price of electricity.

This affects the basic supply plan. This is the normal plan for customers without a special contract. At Vattenfall, one kilowatt-hour will then cost 42.44 cents instead of 37.93 cents. That is about twelve percent more. A normal household will then pay about 8.27 euros more per month. Vattenfall only lowered its prices in April. Customers will get a letter in December.

Vattenfall says: The cost of power lines has gone up. Buying electricity is also more expensive. The government may soon lower the tax on electricity. Vattenfall will then pass the money on to its customers. But many customers in Berlin already have cheaper contracts. They have a price guarantee. So nothing will change for them for now.

At Gasag, the price per kilowatt-hour is going up by 2.98 cents. The basic charge is also going up by 2.38 euros per month. People always pay this charge, no matter how much gas they need. Gasag is raising it for the first time in 20 years. The company gives two reasons: the war in Iran and more expensive gas lines. Many customers only use gas to cook and heat water. They will pay almost ten euros more per month. People who also use gas to heat their homes will pay about 32 euros more.

Electricity prices are also going up in other parts of Germany. The website Verivox shows this. People can compare prices there. Verivox counted 329 price increases, with an average rise of eight percent. About 3.7 million households will have to pay more because of this.
