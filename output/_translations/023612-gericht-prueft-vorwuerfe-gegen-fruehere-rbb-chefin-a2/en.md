A trial against Patricia Schlesinger is now starting in Berlin. She was formerly the head of the broadcaster RBB. Three other former employees are also on trial.

The RBB makes radio and television programmes for everyone. People pay money for the broadcaster. The public prosecutor's office is taking the four people to court. It says: In 26 cases, they caused the broadcaster to lose money.

According to the public prosecutor's office, some people received about 933,500 euros in extra money. In its view, the payments were not allowed. There was also extra money for the people in charge of the ARD. The ARD is a group of several broadcasters. The RBB was in charge at the time.

According to the public prosecutor's office, Schlesinger and the defendant Wolf-Dieter Wolf broke rules. They did not ask the supervisory board about extra payments. This board checks the work of the people in charge of the broadcaster.

The trial is also about two employees. They retired early or stopped working. According to the public prosecutor's office, this cost the RBB about 700,000 euros.

The public prosecutor's office also mentions ten dinners at Schlesinger's home. The RBB paid 4,871 euros for them. A trip to London with her husband cost the broadcaster about 1,651 euros. Schlesinger says: The dinners were for work. The other defendants also say: The accusations are not true.

The problems at the RBB began in the summer of 2022. First, there were reports about a contract for Schlesinger's husband. After that, there was also criticism about the money for the people in charge. Schlesinger resigned in August 2022. Later, the RBB fired her without a waiting period.

The court has planned 56 days for the trial.
