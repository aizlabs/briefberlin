In Berlin, SPD, Grüne and Linke are talking about a new government. But the state of Berlin has little money. That is why the money experts from the three parties have now made clear rules.

The experts sent a text to all the groups in the talks. It is called “Budget Instructions”. The budget is the plan for the state's money. The rule is simple: Every new project needs a sure way to pay for it. The money can come from another area. Or the state gets new income.

First, the parties want to make careful plans for their first agreements. These projects cost about 220 million euros per year. They include a limit on rents in homes owned by the state. The low-cost ticket for people with little money should cost only nine euros. The parties are also planning a free Sunday at museums, more bike lanes and more bus lanes.

Many politicians have long wish lists. But anyone who wants a new project must also save money. They must say: We will spend less money here. The experts are thinking mainly of CDU projects. The CDU is in the government until now. Small cuts are good too. They show: The money really reaches the people.

But the groups cannot say: We will pay for this with higher taxes. The experts say: Berlin already needs this money for the whole budget. A project can go ahead without new money only in a few cases, when there are good reasons.
