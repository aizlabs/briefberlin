Many households in Berlin will soon have to pay more for energy again. Following the gas supplier Gasag, Vattenfall is now also raising its basic supply prices. This is the standard tariff for customers without their own contract. From 1 February 2027, a kilowatt-hour of electricity on the Berlin basic residential electricity tariff will cost 42.44 cents instead of 37.93 cents. That is about twelve percent more, even though Vattenfall only lowered its prices in April. An average household using 2,200 kilowatt-hours a year will pay around 8.27 euros more per month in future. However, the fixed base price will stay the same. Customers will receive a letter with all the information in December.

Vattenfall gives two reasons. Grid fees, the charges for using the electricity lines, have risen. They make up a large part of the price of electricity. The company is also paying more to buy electricity. If the government lowers the electricity tax, Vattenfall will take this into account in the annual bill, as required by law. However, the company stresses that most customers in Berlin have long been on cheaper special tariffs. They will continue to have a price guarantee.

Gas customers on the basic supply tariff will also pay more from January. Gasag is raising the price per kilowatt-hour by 2.98 cents. The monthly base price is rising by 2.38 euros – for the first time in twenty years. Gasag says the reason is the war in Iran, as it continues to put pressure on the international trade in liquefied gas. Grid fees are also rising in this case. Gasag lowered its prices at the start of 2026. Despite the difficult situation on the energy markets, they then remained stable for a long time. Around 60 percent of customers use gas only for cooking and hot water. Using 3,000 kilowatt-hours a year, they will pay just under ten euros more per month. Someone who also heats a standard apartment with gas uses about 12,000 kilowatt-hours. They will then pay around 32 euros more per month.

Berlin is not an isolated case. According to an analysis by the comparison website Verivox, regional basic suppliers have so far announced 329 electricity price increases for December to February. On average, prices are rising by eight percent. An estimated 3.7 million households are affected. The suppliers include EnBW, EWE, Rheinenergie and Mainova.
