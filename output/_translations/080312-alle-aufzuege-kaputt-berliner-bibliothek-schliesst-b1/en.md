The Amerika-Gedenkbibliothek (AGB) in Berlin-Kreuzberg has closed its popular salon to visitors for the time being. The reason: All the lifts in the building are broken, including the last backup lift, which was the most recent to break down. A new lift is not yet working. The closure fell on the nationwide reading day, of all days, when many families traditionally visit libraries. The salon is on the ground floor next to the entrance, behind a large glass wall. Guests normally drink coffee, read newspapers or attend events there. The art lending library is also there: people can borrow paintings and sculptures for free. It is currently closed, and the magazines are also unavailable.

Without lifts, staff can no longer transport books and other media between floors. As a result, the whole operation is slower and orders take longer. The Zentral- und Landesbibliothek Berlin (ZLB) is now using the salon as a place for returns and collections. People in wheelchairs can also no longer reach the offices. The ZLB warns that it may have to close the site temporarily if there are further problems. On top of that, around 3,000 people visit the building at Eva-Mamlok-Platz every day, although it was designed for only 500 visitors.

The library management sees the breakdown as the result of a long-standing problem: many necessary repairs and renovations have been waiting for years. Technical problems are becoming more frequent, and many problems remain even after repairs. Staff have been dealing with the defects for a long time. When it rains, they place buckets under leaks, and they hand out earplugs because of the construction noise. They dry wet books and carry media across several floors. ZLB director Jonas Fansa is therefore calling on politicians to make a quick decision. Berlin needs a functioning central library at last, he says. Otherwise, the city risks the library having to close forever.

A move to Alexanderplatz has long been seen as a solution. In September, the state of Berlin and the company Commerz Real signed a joint letter of intent. Commerz Real owns the Galeria building at Alexanderplatz 9. Both sides want to bring the ZLB's two sites together there. The Galeria department store is to remain in part of the building, however.
