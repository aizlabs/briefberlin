On Sunday, the police found 13-year-old Lisa T. The girl from Berlin was dead. She was in an empty house in Altglietzen in Brandenburg.

Lisa had been missing since Tuesday. That morning, she was on her way to school. After that, many people looked for her. Her mother asked for help on the internet.

On Sunday at around 12 noon, the police got a report. Someone had gone into the empty house without permission. So the police went to the house. They found Lisa there by chance.

The search for Lisa did not lead the police there. Altglietzen is near the border with Poland. The town is about 50 kilometers from Berlin-Marzahn. Lisa lived there.

A badly injured man was also in the house. According to the public prosecutor's office, he had hurt himself. The police believe he took Lisa away and killed her. They are now checking whether it was murder.

The man was taken to hospital, and the police are guarding him. His life was in danger. At first, it was not clear who he was. It was also not yet known how Lisa died.

A doctor is due to examine her body on Monday. The police are looking for clues in the house. They want to know: How did the man take Lisa away? The time leading up to her death is also important.
