The party Die Linke in Berlin has kicked politician Vanessa Emde out of its parliamentary group. A parliamentary group is the group of politicians from one party in parliament. The group voted on it on Tuesday.

Emde has faced a lot of criticism for weeks. She has spoken very negatively about Israel. In October, she signed a text against hatred of Jews. But two days later, she publicly took back her signature. That is why the leaders of the group called for her removal two weeks ago.

33 members voted to remove her. Six members were against it. Four members did not vote yes or no. The group now has only 43 members.

Die Linke wants to form a government with the SPD and Grüne. But the SPD and Grüne said: We will not do that with Emde. Now they are happy. The Berlin SPD leader Steffen Krach says: This is an important success against hatred of Jews. The three parties also want to include the protection of Jews in their agreement.

Josef Schuster is the head of the Zentralrat der Juden. This group speaks for Jews in Germany. He says: This is only a first step. Emde is not the only person with such views.

Emde does not agree with the decision. She says: The media are running a campaign against me. The group only did what the SPD and Grüne wanted. But Emde is staying in parliament.
