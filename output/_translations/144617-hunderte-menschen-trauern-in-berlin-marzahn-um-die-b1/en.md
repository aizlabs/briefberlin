Several hundred people in Berlin-Marzahn have said goodbye to Lisa T., the schoolgirl who was killed. Many held flowers and were visibly moved. The funeral service for the 13-year-old was private. It took place at a funeral home that says it has organised services for the Vietnamese community for years. Exactly two weeks earlier, Lisa’s body had been found in an abandoned building in Brandenburg.

According to police estimates, about 500 mourners arrived in the morning. However, they could only enter the building gradually. One woman carried a cushion with a picture of the schoolgirl. Another woman explained to her young son that the people were there to say goodbye. Even passers-by stopped briefly. Police were at the scene with 60 officers and reported that everything was completely peaceful, with no disturbances. According to media reports, Lisa is to be cremated after the service and buried in Germany.

A week earlier, dozens of people had remembered the girl with Vietnamese roots at the village church in Altglietzen. The village is part of Bad Freienwalde, northeast of Berlin. Police had found the body there on 8 November.

How Lisa died is still unclear. The autopsy, a medical examination of the body, has not provided an answer either. The public prosecutor’s office has therefore ordered further examinations, but the results are not yet available. The suspected perpetrator was a 22-year-old German man. Police arrested him at the scene where Lisa was found, when he was seriously injured. He later died from a gunshot wound to the head. According to the Berlin public prosecutor’s office, he had shot himself. Investigators are also checking more than 200 tips received by police after Lisa disappeared.

Lisa was reported missing in Marzahn on 3 November. A large search then began, involving hundreds of emergency workers, sniffer dogs, a helicopter and even an underwater drone. The breakthrough finally came on a Sunday, following a report of possible trespassing. Someone may have entered a house without permission. This report led the officers to the empty house in Brandenburg.
