A man protested outside the Bundestag on Sunday morning. The Bundestag is the German parliament in Berlin. After the protest, politicians are talking about security there.

The 22-year-old climbed up a tall flagpole. There he lowered the German flag. It fell to the ground. Then he raised a Palestinian flag.

The pole is almost 30 meters tall. The man was there for more than an hour. Two people helped keep him from falling. The fire service used a long ladder. They wanted to use it to reach the man.

Then the man climbed down by himself. The police first arrested the three people. They checked their names and details. After that, they continued to hold the climber. Other emergency workers removed the Palestinian flag.

The police are now looking into several possible crimes. For example: Did the three people insult German state symbols? Did they damage anything or enter an area without permission? There are also rules for gatherings. The police are checking: Did the protest break these rules?

The pole was apparently damaged. Workers first repaired it as best they could. The German flag is now flying there again.

The grounds around the Bundestag are open to the public. The Berlin police protect the building from the outside. But they cannot guard the pole all the time. They now want to check the security there.

Bundestag president Julia Klöckner is calling for more protection. Berlin should also help with this. CDU politician Thorsten Frei wants to look into a special protection zone. Demonstrations are forbidden there. Special rules for gatherings already apply at the Bundestag. Until 1999, there was such a protection zone around the parliament in Bonn.
