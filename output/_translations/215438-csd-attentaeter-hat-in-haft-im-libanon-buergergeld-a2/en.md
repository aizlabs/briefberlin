Abdul B. attacked Christopher Street Day in Berlin in July 2026. CSD is a big celebration for queer people. Now it is clear: He received money from Germany even while he was in prison in Lebanon.

The money is called Bürgergeld. People with little money get it from the state. From May to November 2025, Abdul B. received 790.61 euros every month. The Ministry of Labour gave these numbers. René Springer, a politician from the AfD, had asked for them.

Abdul B. is an Islamist. On 20 May 2025, he travelled to Lebanon. His plan was to join the terrorist group Islamischer Staat. In July, the authorities in Lebanon arrested him. A court there found him guilty. On 17 November 2025, Lebanon sent him back to Germany.

The German embassy visited him in prison. But the job centre knew nothing. The job centre is the office that handles benefits. News reports say: The job centre often invited him to appointments. But then sick notes came from Germany. Who sent them? Which doctors wrote them? No one knows yet.

Abdul B. had been receiving money from the state since January 2015. He was still a child then. From November 2025 to May 2026, he was in prison in Germany. During this time, he received no money. In May 2026, a court found him guilty. After that, he was free at first. That is why he started receiving money again on 12 May. For that month, it was 524.55 euros. From June, it was 804.50 euros a month.

On 25 July 2026, he attacked the CSD. A woman died. More than 24 people were injured. Some are very seriously hurt. One day later, the police shot him dead.

Springer strongly criticizes the state. He says: The state made very serious mistakes. He especially criticizes Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt of the CSU. Springer says: Dobrindt always speaks very firmly about safety. But his offices did not tell the job centre. Now many people are asking: How can security offices and offices that help people in need work together better?
