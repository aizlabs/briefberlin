Steffen Krach is the leader of the SPD in Berlin. The prosecutor's office in Hanover will soon be allowed to investigate him again. This means: It is looking for information about possible crimes.

Krach recently became a politician in the Berlin parliament. Politicians there have special protection. That is why the prosecutor's office took a break. On 3 November, it asked the parliament for permission. The president of the parliament is Hendrikje Klein from the party Die Linke. She has looked into the case and has no objection.

This is mostly a formality. The rule is this: After 48 hours, the prosecutor's office may continue its work. But this only applies if the president does not say no. The exact date is not yet clear. For charges to be brought in court, a group of politicians in the parliament must decide later.

Krach used to be president of Region Hannover. He also oversaw a large hospital company. This company sold an old hospital site in Lehrte. The prosecutor's office believes: Krach passed on secret information about the sale. He may also have received benefits in two cases. Krach says: That is not true.

Two other SPD politicians are now also in trouble. The prosecutor's office believes: They helped with the acts. Stefan Politze was head of the SPD group in the state parliament of Lower Saxony. He left this job at the beginning of October. Adis Ahmetović from Hanover is a politician in the Bundestag. He may have helped with illegal agreements. These also involve secret information from companies. Both politicians say: We did nothing wrong.
