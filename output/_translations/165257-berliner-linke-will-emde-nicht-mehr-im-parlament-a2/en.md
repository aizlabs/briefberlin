Berlin Die Linke wants Vanessa Emde to give up her seat. Emde is a politician from Die Linke from Neukölln. She sits in the Berlin parliament, the House of Representatives.

The leadership of the Berlin party decided on Friday evening: Emde should leave. This seat in parliament is called a mandate. Before the decision, there was a long argument in the party.

The party gives simple reasons for this step. In its view, Emde ignored joint decisions several times. She also did not keep agreements with the party. Such rules apply to the party's politicians.

Kerstin Wolter and Maximilian Schirmer lead Berlin Die Linke. They say: The party no longer has trust in Emde. Emde came into parliament directly through votes from people in Neukölln.

In the end, it is clear: The argument in Berlin Die Linke is not over yet. Now it is about whether Emde really gives up her seat.
