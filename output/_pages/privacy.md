## Readers

BriefBerlin is a static site. It does not currently run first-party analytics, active advertising, user accounts, comments, or stored contact forms.

## Newsletter (Brevo)

The daily newsletter is operated through Brevo (Brevo SAS, formerly Sendinblue) as our processor under Brevo's standard data processing agreement. The signup form on the [newsletter page]({{ '/newsletter/' | relative_url }}) is a Brevo-hosted form. When you sign up, the following happens:

- **What is transmitted:** Your email address, the time of submission, and your IP address are transmitted to Brevo. The signup form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile; Cloudflare processes connection data (including your IP address) to validate the challenge before the form can be submitted.
- **Double opt-in:** After submitting the form you receive a confirmation email. Only the click on the confirmation link completes the signup. Brevo stores the signup and confirmation events - including timestamps and IP addresses - as the consent record for your subscription.
- **What this means:** More data is processed than your email address alone. Provider logs include timestamps, IP addresses and consent events, and are retained by Brevo according to its own retention settings.
- **Newsletter delivery:** Issues are sent as Brevo email campaigns to the confirmed subscriber list. Delivery statistics for campaigns are generated by the provider.
- **Retention and leaving:** Your address stays on the newsletter list until you unsubscribe. Unsubscribed and blocked addresses remain on Brevo's internal blocklist so that no further emails are sent to them. You can request export of your consent records (signup/confirmation events) and deletion of your contact data; write to [info@briefberlin.de](mailto:info@briefberlin.de).
- **No sale of data:** We do not sell or share newsletter data with third parties beyond the processing described here.

Limitations we have not yet fully verified are tracked in the repository and reviewed before launch (for example, which statistics Brevo collects for campaigns and how long provider logs are kept). This page is updated when those answers are confirmed.

## Cookies

BriefBerlin does not currently set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. The newsletter signup form is loaded from Brevo's form servers (sibforms.com); check Brevo's privacy policy for cookies or similar technologies set by the form itself.
