Der Estrel Tower <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-1">steht</button> in Berlin-Neukölln an der Sonnenallee. Das <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-2">Hochhaus</button> ist 176 Meter hoch und hat 45 <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">Etagen</button>. Es <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">soll</button> das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-6">höchste</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">Gebäude</button> in Berlin sein.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-7">Seit etwa</button> einem Jahr <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">sucht</button> das Projekt <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-9">Mieter</button> für seine Büros. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-10">Bis jetzt</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-11">unterschreibt</button> aber keine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Firma</button> einen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">Vertrag</button> für diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">Räume</button>. Mit einem <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-15">Mietvertrag</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-16">darf</button> eine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Firma</button> die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">Räume</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-17">nutzen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-18">Es geht um</button> sieben <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">Etagen</button> in dem großen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-19">Turm</button>.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">Dort</button> gibt es <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">zusammen</button> etwa 9.300 Quadratmeter Platz für Büros. Das ist sehr viel Platz für <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-22">mehrere</button> Firmen. Die Firmen können die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">Räume</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">frei</button> für ihre Teams planen. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">Deshalb</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">passen</button> die Büros zu kleinen und großen Teams.

Das Projekt <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">nennt</button> auch andere <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-27">Vorteile</button> für Firmen als <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-9">Mieter</button>. Im Haus gibt es ein Hotel und Restaurants. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">Außerdem</button> gibt es <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">Sportangebote</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">Räume</button> für große <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">Treffen</button>. Auch ein Service am <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">Eingang</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">soll</button> den Firmen helfen. Dieser Service <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">begrüßt</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">Gäste</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">hilft</button> bei Fragen.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">Trotzdem</button> findet das Projekt <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">bisher</button> keine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Firma</button> für die Büros. Der <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-37">geplante</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-38">Einzug</button> in das Haus <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-39">kommt näher</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">Deshalb</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-41">bleibt</button> die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-40">Suche</button> nach Mietern wichtig für den Estrel Tower.
