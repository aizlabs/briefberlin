Die Techno-Clubszene in Berlin und Brandenburg <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-2">erlebt</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">gerade</button> einen großen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Wechsel</button>. Die Menschen, die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">seit</button> den <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">späten</button> 1980er-Jahren und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-6">frühen</button> 1990er-Jahren Partys, Festivals und Clubs <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-7">aufgebaut</button> haben, ziehen sich langsam <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">zurück</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-9">Früher</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-10">begannen</button> viele <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">Orte</button> als spontane und manchmal nur halb legale <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Treffen</button>. Heute sind <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">daraus</button> Firmen, <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-14">Genossenschaften</button>, also <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-15">gemeinsam geführte</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-16">Betriebe</button>, oder Kulturorte mit <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-17">Angestellten</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-18">Miete</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-19">Sicherheitspflichten</button> und hohen Kosten geworden. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">Trotzdem</button> geht es nicht nur um Geld. Es geht auch um Musik, Identität und das alte <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">Versprechen</button> von Freiheit, Toleranz und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-22">Gemeinschaft</button>.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Besonders deutlich</button> sieht man den <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-24">Wandel</button> bei der Nation of Gondwana in Brandenburg. Das Festival <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">gehört zu</button> den <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">ältesten</button> Techno-Festivals der Region. Nach <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-27">rund</button> 30 Jahren hat <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">Mitgründer</button> Markus Ossevorth die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">tägliche</button> Organisation <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">erstmals</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-31">abgegeben</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">Gründe</button> waren auch persönliche <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-33">Schicksalsschläge</button>. Sein <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">Mitgründer</button> André Janizewski <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">starb</button> 2020, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">kurz danach</button> bekam Ossevorth <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">Krebs</button>. Heute <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-37">leiten</button> Aena Spitz und Suse Laukert das Festival in Programm und Organisation. Ossevorth hilft nur noch <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-38">im Hintergrund</button> bei <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-39">Verwaltung</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-40">Büroarbeit</button>. So soll das Festival auch den Kontakt zu jüngeren Raverinnen und Ravern <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-41">behalten</button>.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-42">Ähnliche</button> Fragen gibt es am Berliner Holzmarkt. Dort <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-43">entstand</button> der Club Kater aus der <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-44">früheren</button> Bar 25. Nach der Pandemie kamen viele <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-45">Beschäftigte</button> nicht <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">zurück</button>. Neue Teams waren jünger und hatten andere <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-46">Vorstellungen</button> von Arbeit, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-47">Verantwortung</button> und Clubleben. Auch der Musikgeschmack <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-48">veränderte</button> sich. Der Kater <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-49">stand lange für</button> melodische, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-50">verspielte</button> Musik und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-51">bunte</button> Räume. Viele jüngere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-52">Gäste</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-53">mögen</button> heute <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-54">schnelleren</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-55">härteren</button> Techno. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-56">Deshalb</button> macht die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-57">Clubleitung</button> das Programm <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-58">breiter</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-59">Gleichzeitig</button> will sie die eigene Geschichte nicht ganz <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-60">aufgeben</button>.

Der <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Wechsel</button> zwischen den Generationen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-61">bringt</button> aber Konflikte. Ältere <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-62">Stammgäste</button> sehen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-63">Veränderungen</button> oft <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-64">kritisch</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-65">Zugleich</button> kommen sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-66">seltener</button>, weil <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-67">Beruf</button>, Familie und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-68">Alltag</button> wichtiger geworden sind. Jüngere Menschen sollen die Clubs nun <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-69">tragen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-70">Doch</button> viele können <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-71">wegen</button> hoher <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-72">Eintrittspreise</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-73">Lebenshaltungskosten</button> nicht oft <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-74">ausgehen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-56">Deshalb</button> hat die Szene zwei <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-75">Aufgaben</button>. Sie muss ihre <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">Orte</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-76">wirtschaftlich</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-77">sichern</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-78">Außerdem</button> muss sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-79">offen bleiben</button>, damit junge Menschen die Kultur <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-80">weiterführen</button>.
