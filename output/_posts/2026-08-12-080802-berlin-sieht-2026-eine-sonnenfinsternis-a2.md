Am Mittwoch, dem 12. August 2026, gibt es in Berlin eine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-2">seltene</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Sonnenfinsternis</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dabei</button> steht der Mond vor der Sonne und deckt sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">zum Teil</button> ab. Das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">passiert</button> am <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-6">Abend</button> zwischen 19.15 Uhr und <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-7">Sonnenuntergang</button> um <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">etwa</button> 20.38 Uhr.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-9">Zuerst</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-10">beginnt</button> der Mond, die Sonne zu <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">bedecken</button>. Um 20.08 Uhr ist der <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-12">wichtigste</button> Moment. Dann sieht man in Berlin <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">nur noch</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">etwa</button> 15 Prozent der Sonne. Die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Sonnenfinsternis</button> ist aber nicht total, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">weil</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-15">immer</button> ein <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-16">heller</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-17">Teil</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-18">bleibt</button>.

Die Sonne steht dann schon sehr <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-19">tief</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">Deshalb</button> braucht man einen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-21">freien Blick</button> nach West-Nordwesten. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-22">Hohe</button> Häuser und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Bäume</button> können die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">Sicht</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">verdecken</button>. Gute Orte sind offene <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">Plätze</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-27">Ufer</button> oder <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">sichere</button> höhere Punkte. Wenn das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">Wetter</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">klar</button> ist, sieht der <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-7">Sonnenuntergang</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">besonders</button> aus.

Man <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">darf</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">nie</button> direkt in die Sonne <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">schauen</button>. Auch wenig <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">Sonnenlicht</button> kann den <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">Augen</button> stark <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-37">schaden</button>. Man braucht eine <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-38">geprüfte Brille</button> für Sonnenfinsternisse. Die Brille <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-39">muss</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-40">ganz sein</button> und die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-41">Schutz-Nummer</button> ISO 12312-2 haben. Normale <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-42">Sonnenbrillen</button>, CDs, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-43">Folien</button> oder schwarzes Glas <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-44">schützen</button> nicht genug. Kameras, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-45">Ferngläser</button> und Teleskope brauchen besondere Filter <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-46">vorn</button> am <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-47">Gerät</button>.

Ohne gute Brille kann man die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Sonnenfinsternis</button> auch indirekt sehen. Man kann ein <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-48">kleines</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-49">Loch</button> in Papier machen. Auch ein <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-50">Küchensieb</button> oder Licht unter <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-51">Blättern</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-52">hilft</button>. Dann sieht man eine Form wie ein <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-53">dünner</button> Mond auf einer hellen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-54">Fläche</button>. Die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-55">nächste</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Sonnenfinsternis</button> sieht Berlin am 2. August 2027. Sie ist auch nur <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-56">teilweise</button>. Eine totale <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Sonnenfinsternis</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-57">erwartet</button> man in Berlin vor 2200 nicht.
