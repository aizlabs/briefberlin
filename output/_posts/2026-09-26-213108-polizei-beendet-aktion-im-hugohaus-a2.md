Die Polizei hat am <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-2">Donnerstagabend</button> eine Aktion im Hugohaus in Berlin-Friedrichshain <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-1">beendet</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">Danach</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">kümmerten sich</button> die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">Hausbesitzer</button> um das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">Gebäude</button>.
