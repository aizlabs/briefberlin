Die Polizei in Berlin hat ein Problem bei großen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Kontrollen</button>. Sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-2">nimmt</button> dort oft <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> mit, weil diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> Beweise sein können. Die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Lager</button> für diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> sind jetzt <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">fast voll</button>. Ein <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Lager</button> in der Belziger Straße ist auch <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-6">geschlossen</button>. Dort gibt es <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-7">gefährliche Stoffe</button> im <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-8">Gebäude</button>.

Bei diesen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Kontrollen</button> arbeiten Polizei, <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-9">Zoll</button> und andere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-10">Ämter</button> zusammen. Der <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-9">Zoll</button> ist ein Amt für <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">Waren</button> und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Steuern</button>. Sie suchen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">zum Beispiel</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-14">verbotene</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-15">Spiele um Geld</button>. Sie suchen auch Arbeit <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-16">ohne Anmeldung</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-17">Diebesware</button>, Drogen und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-18">Waffen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-19">Besonders</button> oft <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">geht es um</button> Spielhallen, Shishabars und andere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">Läden</button>.

Das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-22">wichtigste</button> Problem sind große <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Geräte</button> für <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-15">Spiele um Geld</button>. Die Polizei findet sie oft in Spielhallen und Bars. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">Zurzeit</button> kann sie keine neuen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Geräte</button> ins <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Lager</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">bringen</button>. Sie kann alte <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Geräte</button> im <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Lager</button> auch nicht <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">prüfen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-27">Deshalb</button> helfen diese <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Kontrollen</button> vielleicht weniger als <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">geplant</button>.

Das Problem ist nicht neu, aber es ist größer geworden. Schon im letzten Jahr brachten <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">Gerichte</button> Beweise nach Brandenburg. In Berlin-Moabit lagen sehr viele <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> aus alten <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">Fällen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">Dazu gehörten</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">Kleidung</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">Werkzeuge</button>, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">Schmuck</button> und andere Beweise. Viele <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">dürfen</button> lange nicht weg, weil Fälle noch <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">laufen</button>. Auch <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-37">sichere</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-38">Schränke</button> für viel Geld <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-39">fehlen</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-40">inzwischen</button>.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-41">Am Ende</button> will Berlin ein neues <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-42">gemeinsames</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Lager</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-43">bauen</button>. Es <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-44">soll</button> in Treptow bei einem neuen Polizei-Haus <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-45">entstehen</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-46">Bis dahin</button> sucht die Stadt schnell andere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-47">Räume</button>. Der alte Flughafen Tempelhof <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-48">passt</button> dafür <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-49">wohl</button> nicht. Die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-50">Behörden</button> wollen aber weiter solche <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">Läden</button> kontrollieren. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-51">Trotzdem</button> bleibt es riskant, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Dinge</button> im Laden zu lassen. Früher haben <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-52">Unbekannte</button> geschlossene <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-47">Räume</button> und Automaten <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-53">aufgebrochen</button>.
