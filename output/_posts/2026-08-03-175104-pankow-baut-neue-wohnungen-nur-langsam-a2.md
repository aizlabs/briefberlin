In Pankow im <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Norden</button> von Berlin plant die Stadt viele neue <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-2">Wohnungen</button>. Dort <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">wollte</button> Berlin <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">bis zu</button> 2700 <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-2">Wohnungen</button> bauen.
