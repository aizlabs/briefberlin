Ein <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-22">Startpunkt</button> ist der Anleger Havelspitze in Hakenfelde. Ein Anleger ist eine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">Stelle</button>, wo <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">Boote</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">halten</button>. Diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-27">Gegend</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">liegt</button> an der Sigmund-Bergmann-Straße. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">Zuerst</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">fährt</button> die Odin I von der Havelspitze los. Dann <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">fährt</button> sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">unter</button> der Wasserstadtbrücke <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">durch</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">Danach</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">hält</button> sie <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">zum Beispiel</button> in Tegelort und Hakenfelde. Sie <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">fährt</button> auch nach Saatwinkel und zur Insel Valentinswerder. Diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">Orte</button> liegen an der Havel und am Tegeler See.

So <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">verbindet</button> das <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-2">kleine</button> Boot <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-37">verschiedene</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-38">Ufer</button>.
