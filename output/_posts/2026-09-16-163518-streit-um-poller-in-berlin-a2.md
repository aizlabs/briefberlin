Ute Bonde von der CDU ist in Berlin für Verkehr <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-2">zuständig</button>. Sie plant eine neue <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-3">Regel</button> für kleine Straßen in <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-4">Wohngebieten</button>. Ende August <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">bekamen</button> die Berliner <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> den Plan und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-7">kritisieren</button> ihn.

Die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-8">dürfen</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">weiter</button> einfache <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-9">Maßnahmen</button> für <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-10">ruhige</button> Straßen nutzen. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">Dazu gehören</button> kleine <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">Hügel</button> auf der Fahrbahn, Einbahnstraßen und <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">Fahrradstraßen</button>. Solche Regeln <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">sollen</button> Autos <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-15">langsamer</button> machen und Menschen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-16">schützen</button>. Aber Bonde will bei Pollern und festen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-18">Sperren</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-19">strenger</button> sein. <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-17">Poller</button> sind kurze <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">Stangen</button> aus Metall auf der Straße.

Sie stoppen Autos <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">an manchen Stellen</button> in einer Straße. Manchmal blockieren solche <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-18">Sperren</button> einen <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-22">Weg</button> auch <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-23">schräg</button>. Dann fahren viele Autos dort nicht mehr <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-24">weiter</button>. Nach dem Plan nutzen <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> diese <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-18">Sperren</button> nur als <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-25">letzte Lösung</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-26">Zuerst</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">sollen</button> die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> andere einfache Wege <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-27">versuchen</button>.

<button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">Außerdem</button> will die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">Verkehrsverwaltung</button> von Berlin am Ende <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">entscheiden</button>. Diese <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">Behörde</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">kümmert sich</button> um Verkehr in der Stadt. Wenn Polizei oder <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">Feuerwehr</button> Probleme sehen, dann <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">erlaubt</button> die <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-31">Behörde</button> solche <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-18">Sperren</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">meistens</button> nicht. Die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> hatten nur zwei Wochen für ihre <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-36">Antwort</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-37">Deshalb</button> ist der <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-1">Streit</button> in Berlin jetzt groß.

Die <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-6">Bezirke</button> wollen selbst über sichere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-38">Wohnstraßen</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">entscheiden</button>.
